# Music Hub Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Music menu opens `/music/`, one landing page for all music: each live station with ▶ Listen live and what's on now, today's music picks, the Playlists page, and each station's program director and "How it's programmed" pages.

**Architecture:** The page step already builds the strip's data (`stations_json`); the hub is rendered from those same entries plus `latest_picks`, so it needs no new data. The page is static and rebuilt each newscast hour; its "on now" lines are filled live in the browser by the strip's own `stationNow()` in `shell.js`, so the hub, the strip and the player always name the same song (Tarik, Nov 27: "the header song and the player song don't match!!!").

**Tech Stack:** Python, Jinja templates, `templates/shell.js`, pytest, Playwright e2e (`tests/e2e/`).

**Spec:** Tarik, Nov 27: "shouldn't music tab be a landing page for all music content ie picks stations, etc?" Claude's answer (same day): "Music landing (`/music/`, the Music menu): live stations with ▶ and now-playing (Rhythm Lab, ONE Jazz), today's 3 music picks, Playlists, the program director, how it's programmed". Tarik chose the order "Tracklist, Music hub, Archive, then B2". Earlier, on the explainer being buried: the hub "gets a prominent 'How the station is programmed' card".

## Global Constraints

- Scope is the page above. No new data, no new step, no new dependency.
- Text from feeds, Apple and configs goes in as text only: Jinja autoescape; `textContent` in `shell.js`; links through `safe_url`.
- Nothing plays without a tap; one sound at a time (Plan C rules). ▶ uses the strip's existing `play` payload.
- The page states that voices are synthetic and the station is machine-programmed (CLAUDE.md), via the standard footer.
- Our Bauhaus look; KCRW's structure may inform layout, never its look.
- Pick players stay official embeds that load only on tap (music picks rules).

## Review Focus

1. **The listener's player is ahead of the clock:** the hub's "on now" for that station names the player's song, same as the strip (Task 2 e2e `test_hub_now_matches_strip`).
2. **No picks today, or a damaged picks file:** the hub renders without the picks cards and says when picks arrive; never a crash (Task 1 `test_hub_without_picks`).
3. **News and retired stations:** the hub lists only active music stations: Rhythm Lab first, ONE Jazz; not News, not the retired sampler (Task 1 `test_hub_lists_music_stations_only`).
4. **Hostile text in a pick title or a station brief:** shown escaped, never as markup (Task 1 `test_hub_escapes_text`).
5. **The hub reached by a page swap, not a page load:** "on now" lines are filled after the swap (Task 2 e2e `test_hub_now_after_swap`).
