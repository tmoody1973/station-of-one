# Past Newscasts (Archive) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `/archive/` lists every newscast the station has aired, newest day first, each hour playable in place and linked to its page.

**Architecture:** Every hourly build already leaves `data/runs/<YYYY-MM-DDTHH>/newscast.json` on the Sprite, and every hour's audio and page stay in the bucket (checked Nov 27: `audio/2026-11-26T11.mp3` and `h/2026-11-26/1100/` answer 200). The page step reads all of them and renders one static page, rebuilt each newscast hour like the front page. No new data, no new step.

**Tech Stack:** Python, Jinja templates, pytest.

**Spec:** Tarik, Nov 27: "how do I see previous days newscast and how should that be presented using best ux practices". Claude's answer (approved in the order "Tracklist, Music hub, Archive, then B2"): "Newest day first. Each day is a row of its hourly newscasts (time, ▶, lead headline). Previous-day/next-day links, a date picker, and the podcast feed for subscribers. Today stays on the front page."

## Global Constraints

- One page, `archive/index.html`. With two days of history (19 newscasts on Nov 26–27), a section per day with a row of day links at the top replaces previous/next links and a date picker: every day is one tap away. Ruling recorded in the ledger; per-day pages or a picker when the history passes about 60 days (`# ponytail:` comment at the renderer).
- Today is listed too, labelled "Today", so the page is complete; the front page is unchanged.
- Text from posts and scripts goes in as text only (Jinja autoescape). Nothing plays without a tap (the existing `play` macro).
- Times are the station's (Central), from each newscast's id, as on the front page (`_hour_label`).
- The standard footer: voices are synthetic, the show is machine-edited.

## Review Focus

1. **A damaged or half-written `newscast.json`** in a run folder: that hour is skipped, the page still builds (Task 1 `test_archive_skips_damaged_runs`).
2. **Folders that aren't newscast hours** (`test-friday`, `rss-backfill` exist on the Sprite): never listed (Task 1 `test_archive_lists_only_newscast_hours`).
3. **A newscast whose build stopped before the audio was made** (no `audio.duration_ms`): not listed, so no ▶ points at a missing file (Task 1 `test_archive_lists_only_voiced_newscasts`).
4. **Hostile text in a lead headline:** shown escaped (Task 1 `test_archive_escapes_headlines`).
5. **Ordering across midnight and the 12 o'clock hours:** days newest first, hours newest first within a day, noon labelled "12:00 pm" (Task 1 `test_archive_order_and_labels`).
